# Data Visualization and Storyline

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive sales analytics dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The objective is to transform raw business data into meaningful visual insights and communicate the findings through a clear data-driven storyline.

The dashboard analyzes sales and profitability performance across different time periods, categories, sub-categories, regions, and discount levels.

## Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze business performance and identify meaningful patterns in sales and profitability. The analysis aims to support better business decisions through effective data visualization and storytelling.

## Dashboard Structure

### Page 1: Business Overview

Provides an overall view of business performance using key performance indicators and high-level visualizations.

### Page 2: Sales and Profit Trend

Analyzes sales and profit performance across different time periods to identify trends and fluctuations.

### Page 3: Category and Regional Analysis

Examines sales and profitability across product sub-categories and geographical regions to identify high- and low-performing segments.

### Page 4: Discount and Profitability Analysis

Analyzes the relationship between discount levels, sales, and profitability to understand the impact of discount strategies on business performance.

### Page 5: Key Insights and Recommendations

Summarizes the major findings from the analysis and presents actionable recommendations based on the observed business trends.

## Key Insights

- Sales performance varies across different time periods.
- Higher sales do not always result in proportionally higher profits.
- Sales and profit contributions differ across product sub-categories.
- Regional performance varies in terms of sales and profitability.
- Discount management plays an important role in maintaining healthy profit margins.

## Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing and profitable sub-categories.
- Review discount strategies for low-profit products.
- Monitor profit margins alongside overall sales performance.
- Identify opportunities in high-potential regions.
- Use sales and profit trends to support better business planning and decision-making.

## Conclusion

The analysis provides a clear view of the business's sales and profitability performance. It demonstrates that sustainable growth is not only about increasing sales, but also about managing discounts, focusing on profitable segments, and making informed data-driven decisions.
